\subsection{DeformNeXt construction details and limitations}
\label{sec:data-construction-details}
\paragraph{Source semantics and assembly.}
PartNeXt supplies hierarchical semantic labels, not ground-truth mechanical
attachments. We map its annotations to the canonical face array and group
semantic roles before selecting a deformable primary region. Several legs
can share a semantic role while remaining separate physical followers.
For reconstruction, exact coincident vertex classes and connectivity define
numerical physical units. Nearby source surfaces provide frozen
vertex-to-triangle attachment patches, including barycentric coordinates
and rest-frame offsets. The default proximity radius is 0.006 times the
source diagonal, with at most 96 contact samples per component.

The differentiable reconstruction alternates four projected Poisson/rigid
steps; proper rigid rotations use 32 quaternion iterations. A final
Dirichlet solve eliminates rigid follower coordinates. Components in
assembly islands without a deformable primary are fixed. This is a finite
approximation, not an exact nonlinear optimum or a collision guarantee.
Internal vertex coalescing is a numerical operation: the exported candidate
is restored to canonical vertex indices and checked against the unchanged
face array. Canonical source UVs, material assignments and corner attributes
remain the rendering authority.

\paragraph{Personalization and optimization budget.}
The principal donor-reference arm uses eight Cycles RGB images with the
donor's original appearance. A clay-reference arm was exploratory, not the
default for the full snapshot. The donor-specific DreamBooth LoRA uses
800 updates, rank 16, 512-pixel images, batch one, learning rate $10^{-4}$,
constant scheduling, no warmup, gradient accumulation one, and text-encoder
training. Its caption is \emph{A photo of TOK sculpture}; the production
deformation prompt is \emph{A TOK style sculpture of a <category>}.
Personalization starts from pretrained SDXL, not from a diffusion model
trained from scratch on the construction dataset. Donor LoRA weights can
be reused for different sources; source approximate-encoder preparation is
also cached independently of pair optimization.

The main production adaptation uses 300 deformation iterations, resolution
512, batch one, and \emph{cage\_loss\_epoch=83}, with seed 37. The official
configuration used as our baseline has 3,600 iterations, resolution 1,024,
batch four and a cage-loss interval of 1,000. Main/auxiliary SDS weights
change from 10/1 to 2.5/0.25 to compensate the official implementation's
additional batch divisor. This matches the intended gradient scale, not
the stochastic optimization trajectory or total view-sampling budget.
The shortened budget was introduced after small feasibility trials produced
usable final training artifacts. There is no controlled, sufficiently broad
300-versus-3,600 convergence or quality-equivalence study. More iterations
could improve donor transfer or instead exacerbate artifacts. Admitted
early 3,600/600-iteration variants remain in the snapshot with their original
provenance; the snapshot is not a homogeneous 300-iteration benchmark.

\paragraph{Optional refinement and admission.}
Balanced JN-L separately optimizes a coarse deformation field for 120 Adam
iterations at learning rate 0.003. It combines Jacobian and source-crease-aware
normal terms with an area-weighted residual umbrella Laplacian. For a
frozen coarse guide $\bar V$ and residual $r_i=v_i-\bar v_i$, this term is
\begin{equation}
 E_L=\sum_i m_i\left\|r_i-\sum_{j\in\mathcal N(i)}w_{ij}r_j\right\|_2^2.
\end{equation}
Source-graph inverse-edge-length weights are row-normalized, with boundary,
nonmanifold and sharp-crease edges excluded. The latter term's
weight is fixed from the initial gradient-norm ratio to the weighted J+N
terms, without outcome-based weight search. Weak proposal-fidelity/coarse
terms and source assembly constraints stabilize the solve; proposal fidelity
is not a final admission requirement. PA and its refined output are both
retained if independently usable and geometrically distinct. The final
10,165-example snapshot contains 5,611 PA, 4,548 balanced JN-L and six
legacy/bootstrap examples. These counts include different inputs and
configurations and are not a causal comparison of the two arms.

Cheap geometry and deformation diagnostics precede expensive rendering and
encoding. Diagnostic cards use a small fixed RGB subset (typically three
views) and eight clay views from the common 24-camera convention; a full
24-view Cycles sweep is not required for every candidate. Mesh-level
naturalness and view-level edit visibility are assessed separately.
The publication audit read and collated all 10,165 Shape512 and all 10,165
SS examples. Higher-resolution Shape/PBR supervision is prepared separately
for the native cascade used in this paper. Loader success establishes usable
artifacts, not a calibrated naturalness probability. Canonical face
correspondence belongs to the stored mesh pair; it is not asserted for
surfaces decoded by the native VAEs.

All snapshot entries record \emph{human\_review=false}; screening was
model-assisted. Selection-biased quality spot checks do not estimate the
population error rate. Residual small intersections, attachment gaps and
local folds may remain, and ISIR success is not a prerequisite for inclusion.
The construction is intended to supply plausible, visible shape changes,
not certified CAD-quality assemblies. Its source categories are long-tailed
(2--592 edits per category), rather than category-balanced.

\paragraph{Rendering, provenance and split boundary.}
The canonical M0 frame and source UV/materials are shared by M0 and each
final M1. Mesh/attribute, source/donor, configuration and worker hashes,
diagnostics, render evidence and loader receipts accompany the sample
identity. The donor is absent from retargeting inputs: an edited M1 view
provides the desired shape and undeformed M0 views supply appearance.
Exact face/barycentric surface correspondence can supervise matching;
it does not supply ground-truth surface visibility at inference.

The logical split uses seed 20261012 and groups all variants of every source
UID and existing geometry cluster. Orientation-uncertain train-only cases
remain in training. UID and cluster intersections across splits are zero,
but donor/source roles may cross split boundaries. The split is assigned
after the approved snapshot is constructed. It does not establish donor
disjointness, complete semantic-duplicate removal, independence from earlier
multi-view pretraining, or an outcome-blind evaluation of GeoStyle's
population-level feasibility. The fixed indices are mapped by sample
identity; the original physical storage layout is not the logical split.
