\section{Related work}
\paragraph{Image-conditioned 3D generation.}
TRELLIS.2 learns a compact native representation of geometry and material
attributes using sparse voxels~\cite{xiang2025trellis2}.
Pixal3D lifts image features into a back-projected conditioning volume and
extends this mechanism to multiple views~\cite{li2026pixal3d}.
Our implementation continues the supplied multi-view Pixal3D checkpoints
and adapts their source-conditioning path. The separately implemented
multi-view prior and checkpoint lineage are specified in our protocol;
we do not identify every implementation detail with the original paper branch.

ReconViaGen combines reconstruction and generative priors for multi-view
object reconstruction~\cite{chang2025reconviagen}. RecGen3D coordinates
reconstruction and generation within a shared canonical
space~\cite{huang2026recgen3d}; GenRecon uses projected multi-view conditioning
to extend object-level generation to scene
reconstruction~\cite{schmid2026genrecon}. These approaches establish the
importance of conditioning and coordinate alignment. Our experiments focus
on adapting evidence across an intentional edit, rather than claiming
multi-view conditioning or projection itself as a new mechanism.

\paragraph{Image-conditioned editing.}
ShapeUP learns supervised latent translation from a source 3D shape and an
edited image to an edited 3D shape~\cite{gat2026shapeup}.
Our evaluated input instead consists of an edited target image and undeformed
source images with calibrated cameras. This input distinction motivates our
study of appearance evidence under conflicting geometric states. It does not
establish a performance advantage over source-shape-conditioned editing;
no such quantitative comparison has yet been executed in this study.

Direct mesh editing is another related setting. GeoStyle deforms a source
mesh using an image style reference~\cite{choi2026geostyle}; APAP combines
user-controlled deformation with diffusion-based plausibility
priors~\cite{yoo2024apap}. SpaceControl instead introduces test-time spatial
guidance into a pretrained 3D generator~\cite{fedele2025spacecontrol}.
TextureDreamer transfers appearance from sparse reference images to a given
target shape~\cite{yeh2024texturedreamer}. In our evaluated setup the desired
state is supplied through target RGB, and the output geometry is predicted.
A target mesh is used for supervision and scoring, not supplied to the
generative pipeline at inference. These different inputs and objectives
must be reconciled before quantitative comparisons.

\paragraph{Multi-view generation and correspondence-aware aggregation.}
MV-Adapter adds multi-view generation to pretrained text-to-image models and
supports camera or geometry guidance~\cite{huang2025mvadapter}.
MVDiffusion uses given pixel correspondences to exchange information across
views~\cite{tang2023mvdiffusion}. CorrAdapter extracts correspondences from
intermediate diffusion features and aggregates matching regions to improve
multi-image consistency~\cite{zhang2026corradapter}.
Correspondence-guided attention is therefore an established mechanism.
Our study concerns its use in a different setting: observed source and target
images intentionally depict different geometries, and retrieved evidence is
delivered into a pretrained 3D projection-conditioning path. We test candidate
aggregation, the camera-ray sharing unit, and source retargeting separately;
we do not infer their necessity from the existence of correspondence-aware
attention in image generators.

\paragraph{Representations for correspondence.}
Diffusion features can expose image correspondence without task-specific
matching supervision~\cite{tang2023dift}.
Zhang et al. examine complementary correspondence properties of diffusion
and DINO features~\cite{zhang2023twofeatures}.
VGGT-$\Omega$ learns geometry-aware representations through reconstruction
training~\cite{wang2026vggtomega}. RoMa-$\Omega$ distinguishes zero-shot
feature matching from learned matching on feed-forward 3D
representations~\cite{nordstrom2026romaomega}.
These works motivate separating raw descriptor quality, learned matching,
and candidate aggregation. Our experiments additionally ask whether their
correspondence evidence improves generated geometry and source appearance
when delivered through the pretrained projection-conditioning path.
