\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
%
\YW{
We presented \emph{GEAR3D}, a geometry-conditioned image-to-3D framework that combines VLM-guided target-view editing with semantic feature matching, matching-aware feature aggregation, and target-aware feature retargeting.
%
Experiments on \emph{DeformNeXt-10k} demonstrate a favorable balance between
source appearance preservation and target alignment across geometric
variations.

\noindent\textbf{Limitation and Future Work.}
%
Our method relies on reliable target-view editing and semantic correspondences, which may become ambiguous under severe deformation or occlusion.
%
Future work will extend appearance retargeting to topology-changing edits and unmatched regions, while exploring more diverse VLM-guided editing scenarios.
}
